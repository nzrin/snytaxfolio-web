Syntax Folio is a word game. Points, coins, ranks, and badges are parts of the game. They are not money and cannot be exchanged for cash.

1. Using Syntax Folio

These terms apply to the Syntax Folio app and this website, provided by the Syntax Folio developer (“we,” “us,” or “our”). By using them, you agree to these terms. If you cannot legally agree on your own, a parent or guardian should review them with you. Any applicable app-store terms also apply.

You may use the app for lawful personal play. Please do not interfere with the app, website, or services, or try to access another player’s account.

2. Guest play and accounts

You can try the game as a guest for a limited number of phases. An account is required to continue beyond the guest limit, sync progress, and appear on the leaderboard. Keep your sign-in details secure and choose a public username with care.

Guest progress is stored locally. If you create an account on that device, the app can bring eligible local progress into your account. Clearing app data, removing the app, or signing out may remove local progress.

3. Progress and leaderboard

Completing phases can earn points, coins, badges, and a place on the leaderboard according to the game’s current rules. The leaderboard displays signed-in players’ public profile details and scores. We may correct invalid or duplicated scores and change scoring rules as the game develops.

Internet access is needed for account sync and current rankings. Local gameplay may continue offline, with signed-in progress syncing when a connection is available. We cannot promise that every network interruption or device failure will be recoverable.

4. Coins and purchases

Coins are a virtual in-game balance used for listed game actions. They have no cash value and cannot be transferred or redeemed outside the app. Prices and coin quantities appear before you confirm a purchase.

Where coin packs are offered, checkout, billing, refunds, and payment records are handled by the platform’s app store under its rules. The app verifies purchases before crediting coins. A purchase may take time to appear if verification is pending; the app’s Restore Purchases option can check eligible transactions. Coins already spent in the game are not recreated by a restore.

5. Fair play

Please play fairly. Do not use automation, account sharing, tampered purchases, or other methods to manipulate rankings, points, or coin balances. We may restrict an account or correct game records when needed to protect the game and other players, subject to applicable law.

Game sentences and translations are provided for entertainment and language play. They may change as we improve the experience.

7. Availability and changes

We may update categories, phases, rewards, badges, features, and device compatibility. Some services depend on app stores, authentication providers, and internet access. We aim for a reliable experience, but cannot promise uninterrupted service or that every feature will always remain available.

If we change these terms, we will publish a revised version here with a new date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or seek agreement before changes take effect.

8. Signing out and deleting an account

You can sign out or request account deletion in Settings. Signing out clears local account progress from that device. Deleting an account removes access to its synced progress and leaderboard profile. App-store purchase records are controlled separately by the store.

Before deleting an account, spend or review any coins and progress you want to keep. Deleted game progress may not be recoverable.
